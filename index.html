Dot-count estimation task — demo

This is a demonstration of an online experiment on judging how many dots you saw. It runs the real study page from the instructions through the end of the practice block, then stops. It takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

  • Use a desktop or laptop computer with a mouse, in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  • The page asks for full screen and hides the mouse pointer during the trials. Please stay in full screen until the demo ends.
  • Nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere, and there is no payment. The consent page is part of the demonstration.

Start the demo

Each opening shows one of the study's two dot-count ranges at random. To choose one: smaller counts · larger counts.
