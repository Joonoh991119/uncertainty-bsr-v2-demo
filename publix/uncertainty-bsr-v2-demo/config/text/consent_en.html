Instruction and Consent for a Research Participant (for adult participants)

Title of the research: Deciphering the neural sources of adaptive history effects in human cognition through the disentanglement of sensory and memory neural representations of external stimuli-1

Principal Investigator: Prof. Sang-Hun Lee, Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences, College of Natural Sciences, Seoul National University (visionsl@snu.ac.kr)

This research is about how the human brain’s sensory and memory systems form and separate neural representations of external stimuli. You are being asked to participate because you have volunteered to take part in an online behavioral experiment. The research will be conducted only with your voluntary consent. Please read the following information carefully before deciding. If you have any questions, the investigator will be happy to explain in detail.

Why is this research being conducted?

This study is an experiment designed to examine what kinds of judgment or memory errors occur when multiple stimuli are presented sequentially and participants evaluate and remember their characteristics.

How many people will participate in the research?

A total of approximately 6,000 adult participants, aged between 18 and 35, with normal (or corrected-to-normal) vision, will take part in this study.

How will the research proceed?

If you agree to participate, you will perform a visual decision-making task displayed on a screen for about 60 minutes on an online platform (Prolific) after you give consent to participating the experiment. You will be participating the experiment for one to three sessions.

In this study, three types of visual judgment tasks are used.

In the first type of task, two visual stimuli are presented on the screen either at the same time or with a short delay, and you will compare their features.

In the second type of task, the time interval or the difficulty of the stimuli (such as the clarity of the pattern) is adjusted to examine how these changes affect visual judgments.

In the third type of task, simple feedback (correct/incorrect) is provided in addition to these visual judgment tasks.

All three tasks share a similar basic procedure, in which simple visual images are presented on the screen and you compare or judge their features. You will participate in one of these task types, and you may choose to participate in more than one if you wish.

In each trial, you will make one judgment (e.g., “Is the first striped pattern rotated clockwise relative to the second one?”). This process will be repeated multiple times with varying stimuli. The type of stimulus may vary depending on the experiment — for example, it could be an artwork, a face, or a pattern of stripes.

What is the duration of participation in the research?

You will be participating the experiment for one to three sessions. Each participation session will take about 60 minutes.

Once participation in the research has begun, is it possible to stop participating?

Yes. You can stop participating in the research whenever you want with no disadvantage. Any time you want to stop, please tell the investigator immediately. If you wish to stop participating, simply close the experiment window and return to the online platform (e.g., Prolific). Data collected up to that point will remain stored on the server but will not be analyzed or used for research. Upon request, all related data will be permanently deleted. If you withdraw partway through, you may receive only partial compensation or no compensation, depending on platform policy.

Are there any side effects or risks involved in participation in this research?

You will simply view a computer monitor in a comfortable posture for about 60 minutes. Therefore, significant risks are not expected. Nevertheless, should you experience any risk, discomfort, or distress for any reason during the course of your participation in this study, please notify the research investigator immediately.

Are there any advantages to participating in the research?

You will not receive any direct advantage. However, the information you provide will be helpful to promoting understanding of how the brain processes sensory and memory representations.

Are there any disadvantages to participating in the research?

You are free to agree or decline to participate in the research. There will be no disadvantage if you decide not to participate.

Is the information gathered during the participation secure?

The person in charge of managing the personal information collected for this research is Prof. Sang-Hun Lee (visionsl@snu.ac.kr), Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences at Seoul National University. The personal information collected in this study is age, gender, and nationality. Such personal information is only allowed to be accessed by Jaeseob Lim, Juhyoung Ryu, Minjin Choe, Sungje Kim, Minsu Yeo, Joonoh Park, Boyun Lee, Saemi Jung, Jungye Kim, Bohyun Lee and will be stored in the manner described in a locked cabinet in the PI’s office. The consent form will be stored for three years in accordance with the relevant laws and then discarded. The research data will be kept as permanent as possible by the Seoul National University Research Ethics Guidelines. All possible measures will be taken to secure and protect all personal information gathered while proceeding with this research. When the research is reported in an academic journal or presented at a conference, your name and other personal information will not be presented. However, if required by law, your personal information may be provided. In addition, the Seoul National University Institutional Review Board may directly access the research results within the scope of the relevant regulations to inspect whether the research has been conducted without the participant's personal information security and verify the reliability of the research data. In addition, research data excluding personally identifiable information may be reused in other studies conducted by the PI for purposes such as secondary data analysis and methodological validation. Such data may also be shared on online research platforms used by researchers and may be reused by other researchers. If you withdraw participation, you may close the experiment window. Data collected up to that point will remain stored on the server but will not be analyzed or used for research. All related data will be permanently deleted upon your request. By checking the “I agree” box, you acknowledge that you have been informed of all necessary information related to the research in advance and will be deemed to have consented to it.

How much will participants be paid for participation?

Participants who complete the experiment faithfully will receive compensation (9 pounds per hour) according to the policy of the online research platform (e.g., Prolific). If you withdraw partway through, you may receive only partial compensation or no compensation, depending on platform policy.
